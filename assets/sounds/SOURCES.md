# Additional 18 music alarms

Retrieved 2026-10-24. These eighteen additions are different compositions across piano, guitar, jazz, ambient, retro chiptune, synth, hip-hop bass, rock and marimba. The three original core WAV files remain unchanged. Every added file is a continuous initial excerpt of its own composition, not a repetition, retuning or alternate slice of another listed work. They are music excerpts for repeating alarms with a short pause, not a claim of sample-perfect seamless looping.

All are creator-submitted OpenGameArt assets whose actual License field was checked for CC0; collection names/search snippets were not treated as licensing evidence. For multi-license listings (Quirky Jazz and Feel Good Island), the expressly offered CC0 option is selected. No account or credentials used.

Processing: read the full source download; take from 0 seconds for up to 20 seconds; EBU R128 loudness normalization target -20 LUFS, true-peak target -3 dBTP, LRA target 7; 40 ms opening fade and 350 ms ending fade; convert to stereo 44,100 Hz signed 16-bit PCM WAV. Shorter works are retained whole. Musical phrase boundaries were not subjectively auditioned, so edge fades make a conservative excerpt ending; no tempo/pitch change or single-note concatenation.

FFmpeg was used for conversion; no machine-specific installation path is required.
